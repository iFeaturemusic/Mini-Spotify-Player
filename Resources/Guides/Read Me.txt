MiniPlayer – Spotify in your menu bar
=====================================

Requirements
- Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or later
- Spotify Premium

Install
1. Drag MiniPlayer.app into your Applications folder and open it.
   The app is notarized by Apple. The first time, macOS only asks whether you want to
   open an app downloaded from the internet: click "Open".
2. No window opens and there is no Dock icon: MiniPlayer lives in the menu bar at the
   top right (four bars or a music note). Click it.

Set up (once)
The app needs a Spotify "Client ID". There are two ways to get one:

  A) Your own Spotify developer app (about 2 minutes)
     1. Open https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard → "Create app"
     2. Any name and description; set the redirect URI to exactly:
        http://127.0.0.1:8898/callback
     3. Tick "Web API", save, and copy the Client ID.

  B) The Client ID of the person who sent you the app
     They need to add your Spotify email address under "User Management"
     in their developer dashboard.

Then:
1. Paste the Client ID into MiniPlayer → "Continue"
2. "Sign in with Spotify" → confirm in the browser
3. Click "Activate" on the "Built-in Player" card → confirm once more in the browser
Done: pick a song and MiniPlayer plays it by itself (no Spotify app needed).

Tips
- The interface is in German or English: gear icon next to the tabs → Language.
- Gear icon: settings for language, start with your Mac, visualizer, window on top, account.
- Right-click the menu bar icon: settings, pop out as a window, sign out.
- Speech bubble on the cover: lyrics to sing along.
- Arrow icon on the cover: share the song.
- Next to the volume: hide the library (cover only) and pop the player out into a window.
- Drag the edges of the popped-out window to resize it.
